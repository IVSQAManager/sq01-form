Supplier Qualification Assessment

This SQ-01 form is designed to streamline our vendor onboarding and evaluation process. Please review the following instructions carefully to ensure a smooth and accurate submission.

1
Getting Started
Click the "Start" button below to begin. You will first be prompted to select the primary product or service your company provides. If your specific offering is not listed, please select "Other" and provide a brief description.
2
Section 1: General Information & Certifications
Please complete all required fields, which are marked with a red asterisk (*).

Have an active QMS Certification? If your organization holds a valid ISO, API, or other recognized Quality Management System (QMS) certification, please upload your active certificates at the bottom of this section. Uploading valid certificates exempts you from having to complete Section 2.

No active certification? If you do not hold formal, active certificates, please proceed to and complete Section 2.
3
Section 3: Self-Assessment Audit
This section consists of a brief self-evaluation regarding your internal processes. There are no right or wrong answers; we simply ask that you provide accurate and transparent responses to help us accurately assess your operational capabilities.
4
Finalizing Your Submission
Once you have completed all required sections, click "Submit SQ-01" at the bottom of the final page. Your responses will be sent directly and securely to the Infinity Valve Quality Management Team. No files need to be downloaded or emailed — your submission is received instantly.

Thank you for your time and cooperation. We look forward to the opportunity to do business with you.

Renewal Assessment

Infinity Valve & Supply, LLC
Supplier Qualification Renewal (SQ-01)

As part of our Quality Management System requirements, it is time for your standard four-year supplier re-evaluation. We value our continued partnership and have streamlined this process to save you time.

When you click "Start" below, you will find that your form has been pre-populated with the data from your previous assessment. Please follow these brief instructions to complete your renewal:

1
Verify General Information
Please review Section 1 to ensure your company details, primary contacts, and the services/products you provide are still accurate. Make any necessary updates directly in the fields provided.
2
Update Quality Certifications (Important)
If your organization holds an ISO, API, or other recognized QMS certification, please upload your current, active certificates at the bottom of Section 1.

Note: If your previous certificates have expired and you do not have active replacements, you will be required to complete Section 2.
3
Review Self-Assessment Responses
If applicable, please review your previous answers in the Section 3 self-audit (and Section 2, if you do not hold active certifications). Ensure these responses still accurately reflect your current internal processes and update any answers as needed.
4
Finalize and Submit
Once you have verified all information and attached any updated certificates, click "Submit SQ-01". Your updated responses will be sent directly and securely to the Infinity Valve Quality Management Team. No files need to be downloaded or emailed.

Thank you for your time, prompt response, and continued partnership with Infinity Valve & Supply.
